# CAD

3D models, assemblies, and drawings for the mechanical parts.

## What to put here

- **Source files** from your CAD tool, so others can edit them.
- **STEP files** (`.step`) for every part, so people can open them in any CAD tool.
- **STL files** (`.stl`) for anything you 3D print.
- **Drawings** as PDF for anything you cut, drill, or machine.

Name files after the part, for example `motor-mount.step` and `motor-mount.stl`.

## Free tools

- [Onshape](https://www.onshape.com/en/education/) is free for students and runs in the browser. Since Onshape files live online, put a link to your document here and export STEP/STL files into this folder.
- [FreeCAD](https://www.freecad.org/) is free and open source.
- [Fusion](https://www.autodesk.com/education/edu-software/overview) is free for students with an education license.
